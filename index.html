+ Who We Are

About Us

Diversified Dynamic Care (DDC) is a healthcare service organization offering services such as Day Program, Developmental Services, Healthcare Staffing, Home Care, Residential Placement, Respite Care, and Advanced Foot Care.

Our healthcare staffing specializes in the provision of nursing professionals to Long-Term Care, residential and community healthcare, and support services to children, seniors, youths with behavioral challenges, and people with challenging abilities (developmental, mental, physical, and dementia).

Our services aim to provide freedom of choice, dignity, and respect, and promote social inclusion for people with differing abilities.
